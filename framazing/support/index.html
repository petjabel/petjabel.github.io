Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to petibel.dev@gmail.com. Please mention your phone or tablet model and what you were doing. We usually reply within a few days.

Questions

Where are my saved photos and cards?

In your photo library, in an album called Framazing. If you gave the app access to add photos only, they are in your library without the album.

How do I print a greeting card?

Choose PDF for printing on the export screen and share it, for example by email to a print shop or to Files. Print it at 100% (actual size). A folded card is printed on both sides of the sheet and folded in half.

Can I print a framed photo at home?

Yes. Choose the print size when you start, then save or share the image. It is made at 300 DPI in the exact size, so print it at 100% without fitting it to the page.

I bought Pro on another phone, or reinstalled the app. How do I get it back?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

What do I get with Pro?

All frames and greeting cards, every print size and folded cards, all shapes, filters, fonts and stickers, up to 3 photos in a design, no ads and no watermark.

Can I use a Pro frame without buying Pro?

Yes. Watch a short ad to use it in that design, or watch one when you export a design with other Pro items. Without an internet connection, the item unlocks after a short wait.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. This option appears where the law requires it, for example in the European Union.

Where are my earlier designs?

Your last three projects are on the home screen under Recent. Older projects aren't kept, so save the ones you like to your photo library.

How do I change the language or print sizes?

Open Settings. The app speaks English and Bulgarian, and offers European or UK print sizes.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens on your device. See the privacy policy.
