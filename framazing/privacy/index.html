Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short: your photos never leave your device. Framazing has no accounts and no analytics. Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which you can limit through the consent choices. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat.

This policy explains how the Framazing app (in Bulgarian: Рамкастично) for iOS and Android handles information. The app is made by Petibel ("we"). If you have a question, write to petibel.dev@gmail.com.

Your photos and designs

The app reads the photos you pick, or take with the camera, and does all editing on your device. Your photos, designs, text and recent projects are stored only on your device. We never upload them, and we can't see them. A design leaves your device only when you save it to your photo library or share it yourself, for example by email or a messaging app.

The app asks for access to your photos (to pick photos and to save your creations) and to the camera (only if you take a photo in the app). You can change these permissions at any time in your device's settings.

Settings

Your choices, such as the theme, the language and the print-size region, are stored on your device. So is a count of app launches and exports, which the app uses to limit how often ads appear.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you choose to watch to unlock a Pro item, an occasional full-screen ad after an export, and an ad card in the frame and card galleries. Pro users see no ads, and the app makes no ad requests for them.

To show ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address, the app and device you use, and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad loads. If you don't consent to personalised ads, you see non-personalised ads, which are based on the app rather than on you. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices.

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for permission to track only through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you don't allow it, your advertising identifier isn't shared. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are set to content suitable for everyone.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat)

Framazing Pro is a one-time purchase. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether you own Pro and to restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase records, a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), your device type and operating system, and your IP address. Your Pro status is also stored on your device, so it works offline. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Frame downloads

Some frames are downloaded when you first choose them. Each time the app starts, it also fetches the current list of frames from the same file host (Cloudflare), so new frames can appear without an app update. These are plain files: the requests contain no information about you and no identifier, though the host, like any web server, sees your IP address. Downloaded frames and the frame list are kept on your device, so they work offline.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't ask for your name or email in the app.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We don't sell your information.

Your rights

Your photos and designs are stored only on your device, so you control them: deleting a project or uninstalling the app removes them. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, you can use the rights the law gives you, such as access or deletion, through their privacy pages or by writing to us, and we will help. In the European Union you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Framazing is for a general audience. Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone, but the app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
